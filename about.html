Who We Are

Welcome to C. Nyalchand & Sons — a trusted name in Mumbai's textile and clothing heritage.

We offer high-quality, affordable clothing and textiles including bedsheets, handlooms, chadar, towels, dhoti, uparna, poplin, sherwani, cambrics, lungi, shawl, kurta, and more.

All our products are expertly crafted to deliver a mix of supreme comfort, unmatched durability, and excellent style at prices that work for everyone.

Our legacy spans generations, built on the foundation of trust, authenticity, and a deep love for Indian craftsmanship. Every product tells a story of skilled artisans and rich cultural heritage.
